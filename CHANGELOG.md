# v1.2.3

- New **Show all modifiers** switch (Click tooltips > Lines), off by default: the tooltip lists every click you've bound, grouped under its modifier (Shift, Ctrl, Ctrl · Shift...), so you don't have to hold keys to see them.
- Only modifier sets with something bound show. Holding a modifier keeps its group bright, its name green (**Held modifier** under Colors), and dims the rest.
- **Amounts in the full list** adds heal amounts, mana cost and casts left to that list (off by default, for shorter lines).
- `/clicktips all` flips it.
- An "Action bar key" click (a mouse button handed to your keybinding, like a Mouse 5 macro) shows with no modifier held, but no longer repeats under every modifier (with **Hide default clicks** on).

Thanks to the player who suggested the all-modifiers view!
